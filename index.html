Why winter is the busiest time for a roof, not the quietest

Rain is what finds a weakness in a roof.

Replacement slows down. Leaks do not.

Nobody plans a re-roof in January. But a roof that has been holding on all summer is the one that fails in the first serious week of rain, and that is exactly when it needs someone who answers the phone.

We work through it

Emergency leak response runs 24/7, all year. A temporary dry-in to stop the water, then the permanent repair once the weather allows.

Ask for an off-season inspection

The cheapest roof problem is the one found in September rather than the one found by a wet ceiling in December. Inspections are free.
